Continuous Assessment :

-> There shall be continuous evaluation of the student during the semester. For evaluation purposes, total marks assigned to each subject shall be distributed as follows:

-> Two Mid Semester Examinations (Minor-1 and Minor-2) with 30% of total marks assigned to the subject. The best marks of one of these two will be considered for the award of sessional marks.

-> Assignments/Class projects/short class tests/MCQ based quizzes/projects/presentations/group discussions/Attendance with 20% of total marks assigned to the subject.

-> One End Semester Examination (Major Examination) with 50% of total marks assigned to the subject. It is compulsory to appear in the End Semester Examination and secure at least 20% marks of total End semester exam marks. If a candidate secures less than 20% marks of total End semester exam marks, he/she will be awarded an F grade.

Method for the Award of Grades :

--> For the award of grades in a course, all component-wise evaluations shall be done in terms of marks. The components include:

Midterm-1 and Midterm-2 examinations,
Assignments/projects/class presentations/Attendance, and
End semester examination as per regulation.
After converting the marks obtained into percentages, the grades will be assigned as per the guidelines given below:
